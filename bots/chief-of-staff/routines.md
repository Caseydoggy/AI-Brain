# Boss Lady: routines

A routine is a job this bot does by itself on a schedule. Routines run inside Grok. This file is
the record of them, so the broker and every bot can see what runs, and a routine can be rebuilt if
this bot is ever made again. How to set one up: [[skills/set-up-a-routine]].

## Routines to offer

Suggestions, never defaults. Offer the ones the broker wants, at times that suit them, usually
inside their working hours. Rundowns of the day and the week belong to the Daily Brief, inbox
checks to the Inbox Manager, and calendar checks, meeting prep and meeting summaries to Calendar
Watch.

- **Weekly coordination:** read the other bots' logs, see what's stuck or waiting, hand work to the
  right bot by name, and tell the broker only what needs their decision. Often once a week.
- **Priorities check-in:** a short look at open projects and business goals, and what should come
  first. Not a rundown of meetings, emails or the week ahead. Often once a week, or every two
  weeks.
- **Setup follow-up:** check whether the other bots have finished onboarding, whether a needed
  tool is still unconnected, and whether context notes the broker wanted filled in are still
  blank. Offer this only while setup is still underway, then turn it off.
