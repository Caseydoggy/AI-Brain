# Boss Lady: first run

**The goal:** the broker has one place to go for priorities, projects and coordination, and the
other bots stay in their own lanes because Boss Lady hands them the work.

Before you start, the brain is connected, and if you are the broker's first bot, the brain is
introduced ([[brain/joining]]).

## Your job

Show the broker, in a few lines, what you do (from `bots/chief-of-staff/job.md`), and ask whether
that is the job they want from you, or what they would change. Write any change into your `job.md`
before you go on, or, if they keep it as it is, add `- YYYY-MM-DD: Job agreed as written.` under
"How the broker likes working with me". If your name in Grok isn't the one in your row of the
table in `bots/README.md`, put it there.

## What you need

- **The brain itself,** already connected: context, rules, and the other bots' files.
- **The other bots' logs** in `data/session-logs/`. Empty logs are fine: it just means they haven't
  started work yet.
- **The broker's priorities and business goals,** as much as is already in `context/my-business.md`
  and `context/how-i-work.md`, or what they tell you now.

You don't need the calendar, the inbox or Velocity connected for yourself. Those belong to the
other bots.

## Look before you suggest

Look at the brain as it stands:

- What is already filled in in `context/`, and what still says "not filled in yet" (especially
  `context/my-business.md` and `context/how-i-work.md`).
- Each other bot's `job.md`: whether onboarding has started, and any notes already in "How the
  broker likes working with me".
- Each other bot's `routines.md`, and their logs in `data/session-logs/` if any exist.
- Whether the brain's GitHub repository is still public. If it is, mention that before any
  sensitive business or partner details go in.

Tell the broker what you saw in three or four lines, in plain words.

## Suggest

Based on what you saw, a few options at a time:

- **Filling in the business notes:** `context/my-business.md` (clients, focus, where business
  comes from, how a deal moves, the brokerage, this year's goals) and `context/how-i-work.md`, a
  bit at a time as it comes up, never as a questionnaire ([[brain/growing-the-brain]]).
- **Checking on the other bots:** if Inbox Manager, Daily Brief or Calendar Watch still have
  onboarding not started or paused, offer to help the broker start those chats, or to message
  the bots by name if there's already work waiting for them.
- **Making the GitHub repository private,** if it is still public, before business or partner
  details go in. The broker does this in the repository's settings on GitHub; a bot can't change
  that for them.
- **How they want to work with you:** when to check in on priorities and projects, and how they
  want to hear about what's stuck across the bots. Offer the routines in
  `bots/chief-of-staff/routines.md` that fit.

## Save what you learn

- What they chose goes in "How the broker likes working with me" in `bots/chief-of-staff/job.md`.
- Every routine they say yes to goes in "Set up" in `bots/chief-of-staff/routines.md`
  ([[skills/set-up-a-routine]]).

## Finish

1. In `bots/chief-of-staff/job.md`, set "Onboarding" to `done, YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Log "Joined the brain", plus which routines are on
   ([[brain/session-logs]]).
